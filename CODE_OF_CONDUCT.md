# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment or
discriminatory behaviour of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **info@furnas.technology**. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may
remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this code.
